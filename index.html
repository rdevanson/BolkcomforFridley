About Ann

Decades of Service,
Rooted in Community

Ann Bolkcom has served on the Fridley City Council for over 30 years, bringing steady, experienced leadership to the decisions that shape our city.

Her commitment goes well beyond City Hall. Ann has been involved with the Fridley Lions, raising money for charitable causes in the city; has served on the 49ers Day committee, which puts on Fridley’s annual city festival; and has been heavily involved in local arts.

Public Arts Commission

Ann supports Fridley’s new Public Arts Commission. With a long history in local arts, she believes art belongs in our public life and helps make Fridley a community people are proud to call home.

🌳

Parks Revitalization

Ann is a strong supporter of the parks revitalization project, investing in the parks and green spaces where Fridley neighbors gather, play, and connect.

🛡

Public Safety

Ann supports Fridley’s public safety department and the professionals who work every day to keep our neighborhoods safe and our residents protected.

💰

Keeping the Levy Low

Ann is committed to keeping the levy low, so Fridley stays an affordable place for families, seniors, and businesses to call home.
